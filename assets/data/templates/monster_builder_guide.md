# Monster Builder (16×16)

Layer transparent parts at x=0, y=0 without cropping. All parts share a front-facing pose. Suggested order: wings/tail → feet → body → arms → belly/markings/armor → eyes → mouth → ears/horns/crest → crown. Choose one part per slot; ears, horns and crests can overlap. Faces align to eyes at rows 6–8 and mouths at rows 9–11. Bodies occupy rows 4–12 and feet rows 13–15. These are static parts, not animation frames.
